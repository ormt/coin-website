What we paint

Eight living traditions आठ परम्परा

Every commission is painted by hand, start to finish. No prints, no transfers, no generated imagery — the studio signs the back of each work with its atelier mark.

The path of a commission

Four steps, one point of contact

You never have to find, vet, chase or negotiate with an individual painter. That is the studio's job.

Write the brief

Tradition, size, medium, finish and deadline — with a live price as you choose. Ten minutes, no account needed.

We place the work

Our dispatch desk scores every member artist on atelier, grade, workload and deadline, then routes your brief to the best fit.

Sketch, then paint

You approve a sketch before pigment is laid. Progress photographs arrive at each milestone under your order code.

Review & delivery

Studio review against your brief, then framing, crating and insured shipping — in Nepal or anywhere we ship.

Why you deal with the studio

The hand stays behind the curtain

Kalakunja is a collective. Our member artists paint under the studio's name, and their identities stay inside the studio. You get a consistent standard, one contract and one price — and we get to put every brief in front of the right pair of hands.

  • ✣One accountable party. The studio carries the deadline, the quality and the guarantee — not an individual you have never met.
  • ✣Illness, festivals, life. If a hand has to step away mid-commission, we re-route the work internally. Your date does not move.
  • ✣No side channels. Nobody is negotiated down, poached, or pressured off-hours. Our artists are paid a fixed studio rate per commission.
  • ✣You still see the work. Atelier, grade and years of practice are on your tracking page — just not a name or a phone number.

Before you commission

Questions we are asked प्रश्न

Can I choose my artist?

Not by name. You choose the tradition, the finish grade and the deadline — the studio then places the brief with the member artist whose atelier, grade and workload fit it best. If you have commissioned with us before and want the same hand, quote your previous order code and we will route it back to them where their schedule allows.

Why don't you name your artists?

Our members paint under the studio's name so that quality, scheduling and pricing stay the studio's responsibility rather than an individual's. It also protects them from off-hours pressure and price haggling. Their credentials — atelier, grade, years of practice — are visible on your order page.

How many revisions do I get?

Two rounds at the sketch stage, which is where changes are cheap and fast, plus one round of minor colour adjustment on the finished plate. Changes to size, tradition or subject after the sketch is approved are re-quoted as a new commission.

Do you paint religious iconography for non-devotional use?

Yes, and we will tell you plainly when a subject carries ritual meaning that is worth knowing about — consecration, placement, or which forms are traditionally not sold. Our Paubha and Thangka ateliers follow the iconometric proportions, whatever the intended use.

Can you ship outside Nepal?

We ship insured to 31 countries. Works are rolled or crated depending on medium and size, and we handle the Department of Archaeology clearance certificate that Nepali customs requires for paintings. Add export crating in the order form and we will quote the freight before you pay the balance.

What if I don't like the finished painting?

If the finished work departs from the approved sketch and brief, we repaint it at our cost. If you simply change your mind, the deposit covers the hours already worked and we release you from the balance. In eleven years that has happened four times.
